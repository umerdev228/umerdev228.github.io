Case study · Booking SaaS

One platform to run a salon: bookings, payments, WhatsApp and stock

A multi-tenant SaaS for salons, clinics and coaches. Customers book online and pay a deposit, the business chats with them on WhatsApp, and the same system runs the till, staff shifts, inventory and finance. Businesses also get listed on a public marketplace.

The challenge

Small service businesses usually juggle a booking app, a payment link, WhatsApp on someone's phone, a separate till and a spreadsheet for stock. No-shows cost them money, and nothing talks to anything else. The goal was one product that handles the whole customer journey, from discovery to booking to payment to follow-up, for many businesses at once, each with its own staff, hours and rules.

What I built

Booking engine

  • Availability from working hours, staff shifts, leave and existing bookings
  • Google Calendar busy-time respected, so staff are never double-booked
  • Booking state machine, packages and offers
  • Customers reschedule or cancel from a secure link, no login needed

Payments

  • Stripe pay-in-salon, deposit or full prepayment, set per business
  • Webhooks keep booking and payment status in step
  • Finance: cash register, invoices, refunds, commissions, payouts and reports

WhatsApp

  • Shared team inbox on the official WhatsApp Cloud API
  • Approved message templates and broadcast campaigns
  • Automations such as birthday messages

Operations

  • POS, products, inventory, purchase orders, deliveries and stocktakes
  • Staff attendance by kiosk or QR, with shifts and leave
  • Customer CRM with import and duplicate merge, reviews, reports
  • Public marketplace by place and category, plus a coach directory

A TypeScript monorepo: Next.js for the business dashboard and public booking pages, NestJS for a modular API, and Prisma on PostgreSQL. Every query is scoped to its business, so tenants never see each other's data. External services (Stripe, the WhatsApp Cloud API, Google Calendar) talk back through webhooks, so payments and messages update bookings without anyone refreshing.

What this means for your product

Fewer no-shows

Deposits and self-service reschedule links are built into the booking flow.

Customers where they are

WhatsApp is the main channel in the Gulf and South Asia, and it's part of the product, not an add-on.

Built to scale

Multi-tenant from day one, so adding the next hundred businesses is configuration, not code.
