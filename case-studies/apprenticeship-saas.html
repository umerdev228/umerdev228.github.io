Running UK apprenticeships on one platform, for every training provider

A multi-tenant SaaS that UK apprenticeship and T-Level providers use to manage learners from enrolment to end-point assessment: training plans, evidence, progress reviews, off-the-job hours, employers, and a two-way sync with the company's CRM.

The challenge

Apprenticeship funding in the UK depends on evidence: training plans mapped to official knowledge, skills and behaviours, regular progress reviews, logged off-the-job hours, and safeguarding checks. Providers were tracking this across documents and spreadsheets. Each provider needed its own isolated data and branding, and the sales team's CRM had to stay in step with what was actually happening to each learner.

What I built

Portals for every role

  • Admin, centre manager, coach or tutor, learner, T-Level learner, employer, line manager, quality assurer, CRM staff
  • Role impersonation so support can see exactly what a user sees

Compliance workflows

  • Training plans mapped to official standards and KSBs
  • Progress reviews covering safeguarding, Prevent, British Values, and health and safety, with overdue reminders
  • Off-the-job hours, attendance, T-Level timecards with approval
  • Evidence PDF and end-point assessment exports

Multi-tenant platform

  • Provision, suspend and activate tenants, each with its own database
  • Database cloning plus schema compare and drift checks across tenants
  • Master email templates and defaults pushed to every tenant

Two-way CRM sync

  • Learner statuses, product totals per account, employers and line managers flow both ways
  • Contacts and accounts import from the CRM
  • Sync exclusions for records that shouldn't move, and recovery when a sync is paused
  • A comparison command that finds drift before users notice

Each provider runs on its own database, managed from a central admin. Heavy work (reports, reminders, exports and the CRM sync) runs in the background through more than sixty scheduled jobs, so the app stays fast during the day. Schema drift checks keep every tenant database on the same version after each release.
